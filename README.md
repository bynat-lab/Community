## About

This repository is the source of truth for all of our community discussions. Every conversation, idea, and question lives here, so it's open, searchable, and easy to follow.

## How to take part

Head over to the **[Discussions](../../discussions)** tab and pick the category that fits:

| Category | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| 📣 Announcements | Updates and news from the team |
| 💡 Ideas | Suggest features or improvements |
| 🙋 Q&A | Ask questions and get answers from the community |
| 🙌 Show and tell | Share what you've built or learned |
| 💬 General | Anything else on your mind |

Before starting a new thread, search existing discussions. Your question may already have an answer.

## Community guidelines

- Be respectful and kind. Every voice matters.
- Stay on topic and keep threads focused.
- Give context: clear titles and details help others help you.
- No spam, self-promotion, or sensitive information.

## Found a bug?

Discussions are for conversation. If you've found a bug, please open an **[Issue](../../issues)** instead.
